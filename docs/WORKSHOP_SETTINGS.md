# Which setting builds what

Law 25 of `CLAUDE.md`, in full. Written from the island's answer twenty-four (2026-11-01), §5.

**Believed:** the strongest setting is for the hardest code, and a strong builder at the helm
ships a strong venue.

**Measured, on this trunk, in October 2026:** every piece was built well and tested, and the
sum was 67 routes, 37 menu entries, a 12,766 px page under a door, a Swap that opened on
Ethereum for the island's Solana traffic, a cold wallet told "Cannot", and a door that closed
when strangers arrived. None of those is a bug a test catches. They are what a builder model
produces when it also decides: it adds and never removes, it reads an owner's words literally
("like uniswap" became Ethereum first), and it builds the next three things nobody asked for.
The island paid for the same lesson on its own side in August: whole sessions on a builder
model spent on instrumentation no stranger could see, and only the strongest setting could
cut it back.

## The three tiers

**Fable at max, never less:** what is on a stranger's first screen and what comes off it; the
words a stranger reads (Bayla's lines, the hero, every button); anything with money's shape (a
fee line, a ladder weight, the deposit check's open or closed rule, a tier, a pause); the order
things ship in; any screen judged by eye (the sheet, the receipt card, the countdown), drawn on
a canvas before code; the fresh-eyes read at 390 of any build that touched the above, before
it merges.

**Opus at max or extra:** the build of a specified screen to its canvas; the price loop; the
Solana-first defaults; the state machine behind Bayla's line; migrations, runbooks, vault
steps; the red-first test and the fix; the indexer reads. Bounded work with a line that says
done. Its failure mode, the next three things nobody asked for, is contained when the spec
names the one thing and what done means.

**A lower setting:** lint, types, renames, test runs, the changelog line, the em dash count,
heights and word counts at 390, screenshots, dependency bumps, doc moves.

## Done means

Every pull request from here names its setting in the first line of its body. A build in the
first tier that was made without it is reopened. A more capable model changes nothing about
the proof: a screenshot at 390 from a production build (law 6), by any setting.
