# The Tegridy Hundred — top 100 builds toward the one-stop shop

Researched 2026-08-17. Companion to [YEAR_PLAN_2026_2027.md](YEAR_PLAN_2026_2027.md).
Method: six web-grounded research lanes (trading, yield, launch, credit, social, infra) + an adversarial revenue fact-check pass.
Legend: NET+ = genuinely net-positive · DEBATABLE = real value, real harm · EXTRACTIVE = users in aggregate lose (included per request, flagged per house style) · ⚠ = house-law conflict/structural risk · effort S/M/L/XL = days/weeks/months/quarter+.

## Tier S — The money printers

_The twelve highest-revenue builds, ranked by verified comparables. Ten of twelve extend systems the repo already has._

**1. Memecoin Pro Terminal** — DEBATABLE · effort L
   - Live new-pair discovery + one-click trading across both chains, every row pre-scored by the built scanner, deployer-reputation graph, and Heat tiers — the only safety-scored trenches terminal in the market.
   - Earns: 0.75–1% per terminal trade. Axiom: $389M cumulative / ~$139M annualized; Photon $441M lifetime (audited: CONFIRMED). The richest fee category in crypto apps.
   - Replaces: Axiom, Photon, GMGN, BullX, DexScreener

**2. Graduation venue on the native DEX** — NET+ · effort S
   - Auto-migrate every graduated launch into the existing V2-fork DEX with locked LP — wiring, not new contracts.
   - Earns: 0.25% pool fee with 0.05% protocol take. PumpSwap: $608M annualized fees / $110M protocol revenue (CONFIRMED) — where pump.fun doubled its business.
   - Replaces: PumpSwap, Raydium graduation

**3. Ultra-Mode swap fee layer** — NET+ · effort S
   - Default best-execution mode — gasless, MEV-protected, simulated — with a disclosed 0.25–0.5% convenience fee; the raw 0-fee route stays available.
   - Earns: MetaMask took $198.6M cumulative at 0.875% without even best-price routing (CONFIRMED; run-rate caveat ~$51M/yr). Purest extension of what exists.
   - Replaces: MetaMask Swaps, Jupiter Ultra

**4. First-party Solana bonding-curve launchpad** — DEBATABLE · effort M
   - Deploy the already-written curve program as the venue's own pump.fun-class launchpad, Heat-gated, keeping the whole fee instead of the Meteora split.
   - Earns: 1% curve trade fee + creation fee. pump.fun: $800M+ cumulative by Sep 2025, record ~$148M month (audit-corrected figure).
   - Replaces: pump.fun, LetsBonk, Moonshot

**5. Telegram trading bot** — DEBATABLE ⚠ custody risk · effort L
   - Trojan-class TG + PWA bot on venue routing with inline scanner + Heat warnings. Non-custodial session-key design only — the custody attack surface is the house-law conflict.
   - Earns: 0.5–1% per trade. Trojan: $25B lifetime volume at ~1%; Banana Gun ~$2M/week gross at peak.
   - Replaces: Trojan, Banana Gun, Maestro, BonkBot

**6. Creator revenue share** — NET+ · effort M
   - Market-cap-scaled trading-fee share to launch creators, claimable in ETH/SOL, printed on the fact sheet — the anti-rug income stream.
   - Earns: Venue keeps 30–50% of a larger fee base. pump.fun has paid creators $350M+ (CONFIRMED) — proof it drives launch volume, not charity.
   - Replaces: pump.fun creator dash, Believe

**7. Copy-trading engine** — DEBATABLE · effort L
   - Follow smart-money wallets surfaced by the deployer-reputation graph and indexer leaderboards, with size caps, slippage guards, and honest latency labeling.
   - Earns: 1% per copied trade + leader profit-share cut. GMGN: ~$115M annualized, net of referrals (CONFIRMED).
   - Replaces: GMGN, Maestro copy-trade

**8. Hyperliquid builder-code perps desk** — DEBATABLE ⚠ no-perps law · effort M
   - Full perps UI routing to Hyperliquid's book via builder codes — zero contracts deployed, zero oracle risk, no liquidity to bootstrap.
   - Earns: Builder fee up to 0.1% per order. Builder-code pool passed $10M in 2025 (pvp.trade $7.2M alone); HL underlying: $978M annualized fees.
   - Replaces: Hyperliquid app, pvp.trade

**9. On-chain referral engine** — DEBATABLE · effort M
   - Protocol-wide referral links across swap, launcher, terminal: 20–35% of referred fees to the referrer, tiers boosted by veTOWELI lock or Heat standing.
   - Earns: Buys distribution out of existing fees. GMGN's $115M is measured NET of referral payouts; Zora hard-codes 0.3% referral legs into its 1% fee.
   - Replaces: KOL deal-making, ref aggregators

**10. Pooled money market (Aave v3 fork)** — NET+ · effort XL
   - Verbatim Aave v3: blue-chip supply/borrow + flash loans, veTOWELI-boosted rates. The missing lend/borrow pillar at full scale.
   - Earns: 10–20% reserve factor on borrow interest + flash-loan fees. Aave: ~$850M annualized fees / ~$111M protocol revenue.
   - Replaces: Aave, Compound, Spark

**11. White-label launchpad rails** — NET+ · effort L
   - Rent the curve + Heat gate + fact sheets to partner communities as hosted branded launchpads, fee split enforced on-chain.
   - Earns: 20–50% of partner fees. Meteora's partner/DBC line did $78M in FY2025 (CONFIRMED) — the picks-and-shovels version of the launch boom.
   - Replaces: Meteora DBC, launchpad dev shops

**12. Prediction markets** — DEBATABLE ⚠ regulatory · effort XL
   - Binary markets on crypto prices + venue-native events with optimistic-oracle resolution. The regulatory surface is the real cost.
   - Earns: 1–1.75% taker fees. Kalshi: $263.5M fees in 2025 (CONFIRMED); Kalshi + Polymarket cleared >$44B combined volume.
   - Replaces: Polymarket, Kalshi


## Tier A — Flywheel & wedge

_Completes the launch-to-graduation economy and the yield stack — where the Heat/honesty wedge compounds hardest._

**13. Graduation priority auction** — DEBATABLE · effort M
   - Sealed-bid auction for first-block access on own launches — sniper money flows to the token's LP and creator instead of leaking to MEV bots. External sniping only as explicit opt-in.
   - Earns: Cut of auction clearing price + 1% on opt-in external snipes. Banana Gun prices sniping at 2× its manual fee — the demand is proven.
   - Replaces: Banana Gun / Maestro snipers

**14. Isolated lending pairs for launched tokens** — NET+ · effort L
   - Morpho-Blue-fork permissionless isolated markets: any launched token gets an optional lend/borrow pair vs ETH/USDC, risk contained per market.
   - Earns: 5–10% of interest per pair. Morpho: $222M annualized fees. Nobody else offers lending on this venue's launches.
   - Replaces: Morpho, Fraxlend, Euler

**15. Launch-outcome prediction markets** — DEBATABLE ⚠ regulatory · effort M
   - Parimutuel bets on venue-verifiable events — will this launch graduate, will it hold $1M FDV — settled by the venue's own indexer data. No house capital at risk.
   - Earns: 1–2% of winning payouts. Kalshi's 2025 fees were ~1.2% of $22.9B volume.
   - Replaces: Polymarket (crypto events)

**16. Trigger orders (SL/TP/trailing)** — NET+ · effort M
   - Keeper-executed stop-loss, take-profit, trailing, and OCO on any spot token, both chains — extending the live CoW limit orders.
   - Earns: 0.1% per triggered execution (Jupiter Trigger's rate). Stop-losses are straightforwardly protective.
   - Replaces: Jupiter Trigger, BullX auto-sell

**17. MEV recapture auction** — NET+ · effort L
   - Searchers bid for the right to backrun venue order flow; ~90% rebated to users, the rest is revenue. Turns invisible extraction into rebates.
   - Earns: Share of backrun bids (MEV Blocker's model). Context on the pool: Jito tips peaked at $210M in one month (CONFIRMED).
   - Replaces: MEV Blocker, Flashbots Protect

**18. Harvest vaults (auto-compounder)** — NET+ · effort M
   - Beefy-fork auto-compounding over own LP farms, the revenue distributor, and select external pools.
   - Earns: 9.5% of each harvest (Beefy's exact rate) — the most user-aligned fee model in DeFi: no yield, no fee.
   - Replaces: Beefy, Yearn, Kamino

**19. Restaking activation + liquid receipt** — NET+ · effort L
   - Deploy the already-written restaking module and wrap deposits in a liquid receipt earning AVS yield — the cheapest new fee line in the repo.
   - Earns: 5–10% of restaking rewards passed through. ether.fi runs ~$33.6M protocol-wide revenue on this pattern.
   - Replaces: ether.fi, Renzo, Kelp

**20. Curator vault marketplace** — NET+ · effort L
   - Vetted third-party curators run BoringVault-pattern strategies on the venue; the reputation graph is the vetting layer.
   - Earns: Venue takes 20–30% of the curator's 15%-of-yield fee. Gauntlet manages ~$1.88B on exactly these economics.
   - Replaces: Morpho vault frontends, Veda

**21. Curated stable-yield vaults** — NET+ · effort M
   - MetaMorpho-pattern USDC/ETH vaults allocating across own isolated pairs + external blue-chip markets, allocations disclosed per-market.
   - Earns: 5–15% of yield. Morpho ecosystem curator fees grew $2M → $13M during 2025 (+600%).
   - Replaces: Yearn, Summer.fi vaults

**22. Stable yield router** — NET+ · effort M
   - Route stables to the best rate across Morpho/Aave/Kamino with scanner-scored risk labels. The venue issues NO stablecoin — it routes to them.
   - Earns: 10–15% performance fee on yield only. $100M routed at 6% with a 12% skim ≈ $720K/yr.
   - Replaces: CEX earn, protocol frontends

**23. Fixed-yield desk (Pendle integration)** — NET+ · effort M
   - In-app PT/YT access with plain-language fact sheets — including the honest one about YT theta decay.
   - Earns: 0.05–0.15% interface fee on routed PT/YT flow. Pendle: $22.5M annualized fees proves the category.
   - Replaces: Pendle app

**24. Social-attribution launches** — DEBATABLE · effort M
   - Launch tokens attributed to X/Farcaster handles with the creator's fee share escrowed until they verify and claim — honesty-labeled unclaimed attribution.
   - Earns: Curve fee split + expired unclaimed shares. Believe: $6.3M fees in a single 24h (CONFIRMED); Bags ~$16M annualized.
   - Replaces: Bags.fm, Believe

**25. Live launch streams** — DEBATABLE · effort L
   - Video/audio streams attached to launches; creator fee share boosted while live. Requires a real moderation budget — pump.fun's suspension arc is the warning.
   - Earns: Volume uplift on existing fees; pump.fun's stream era drove creator payouts of ~$20M in a week at peak (sub-claims flagged).
   - Replaces: pump.fun livestreams

**26. Rug-refund escrow (launch insurance)** — NET+ · effort M
   - Creator's fee share escrowed for N days; auto-refunds curve buyers pro-rata if deployer wallets dump within the window. Creator-funded, never venue-underwritten — a net-new category and the purest expression of the wedge.
   - Earns: 5–10% of escrowed premiums + launch-share gains from trust. No direct comparable exists (honest gap).
   - Replaces: nothing — net-new category

**27. Anti-snipe decaying-tax mode** — NET+ · effort M
   - Optional launch format: tax starts ~99% and decays to baseline over ~90 minutes, making block-zero sniping unprofitable; proceeds split to veTOWELI + creator.
   - Earns: Venue keeps ~25% of collected tax + standard fees. Virtuals ($39.5M cumulative revenue) pioneered the pattern.
   - Replaces: Virtuals Unicorn, anti-snipe subscriptions

**28. Vesting & lock rails** — NET+ · effort M
   - Sablier-fork streams wired into every launch flow: team allocations and LP provably locked, status printed on fact sheets and read by the scanner.
   - Earns: 0.25% of streamed value + per-claim fees. Honest: Sablier itself earns ~$36K/yr — the value here is trust and launch share, not direct fees.
   - Replaces: Sablier, Streamflow, Team.finance

**29. Afterlife buyback flywheel** — NET+ · effort M
   - Every graduated token gets an optional flywheel address: inflows auto-market-buy and burn (or LP) via the native DEX — transparent, rules-based support instead of dev promises.
   - Earns: 0.25–0.5% on flywheel executions + the DEX pool fee. Heaven hit $1M revenue in 24h with this as its centerpiece.
   - Replaces: Heaven flywheels, buyback bots

**30. One-click leverage loops** — DEBATABLE · effort L
   - Kamino-Multiply-pattern looped positions on majors and LSTs only, conservative LTVs, built on the venue's own lending markets.
   - Earns: 10–25bps origination + elevated borrow spread. Kamino: $63M annualized fees / $10.4M revenue with Multiply as the flagship.
   - Replaces: Kamino Multiply, Gearbox

**31. Liquid locker hub (external ve-tokens)** — NET+ · effort L
   - Convex-pattern liquid wrappers for OTHER protocols' vote-escrow tokens. Never for veTOWELI itself — that would hollow out the venue's own lock.
   - Earns: 10–17% of boosted yield + bribe income through the wrapper. Convex: ~$2.8B TVL and one of DeFi's most durable fee machines.
   - Replaces: Convex, Equilibria, Penpie

**32. LST/LRT yield router** — NET+ · effort M
   - One-click zap into the best-yielding LST/LRT with live peg, liquidity, and slashing warnings from the honesty-gated data layer.
   - Earns: 5–10bps zap fee + partner kickbacks (Sanctum pays partners). Underlying market: Lido alone $690M annualized fees.
   - Replaces: Lido/Jito/Sanctum frontends

**33. towelSOL validator LST** — NET+ · effort M
   - Venue-branded Solana LST via Sanctum's infrastructure, with Heat-tier perks for holders.
   - Earns: Validator + MEV commission on staked SOL. Sanctum: 1,360+ LSTs spawned, $5M+ cumulative partner revenue paid.
   - Replaces: Jito, Marinade frontends

**34. DCA-into-yield** — NET+ · effort S
   - Unfilled DCA balances park in Aave/LSTs until each tranche executes; completed buys can auto-stake.
   - Earns: 10–20% skim of idle-balance yield + 0.1% per fill. Jupiter DCA: $14.7M all-time fees; Jupiter shipped exactly this in 2025.
   - Replaces: Jupiter Recurring, Mean Finance

**35. Bribe market activation** — DEBATABLE · effort S
   - Flip the switch on the DEPLOYED-but-dark VoteIncentives market and aggregate external vote rounds into one claim UI — the lowest-effort new fee line in this list.
   - Earns: 4% of bribe distributions (Hidden Hand's published rate). Votium moved $20M+ per epoch at peak.
   - Replaces: Votium, Hidden Hand, Paladin

**36. BNB launcher expansion** — DEBATABLE · effort L
   - Port the EVM launcher to BNB Chain, where Four.meme periodically out-earns pump.fun and trust tooling would be genuinely novel.
   - Earns: ~1% curve fees + creation fees. Four.meme crossed $1M/day in fees (Oct 2025).
   - Replaces: Four.meme

**37. Base L2 expansion** — NET+ · effort L
   - Launcher + core venue on Base — the roadmap already commits to the go/no-go memo, the island's registry already reads Base, and it is Clanker/Zora territory.
   - Earns: Existing fee lines on cheap gas; Base's launch economy is the second-largest EVM fee pool after mainnet.
   - Replaces: Clanker, Zora (launch side)

**38. Cross-chain one-click swaps** — NET+ · effort S
   - Buy a Solana memecoin from ETH mainnet in one signature via the already-integrated LiFi + Jupiter, with a fee-on-top. The cleanest small win in the list.
   - Earns: 0.2–0.5% integrator fee (LiFi pays it natively). LiFi: $10.6M annualized fees, volume +595% YoY.
   - Replaces: Jumper, Relay, deBridge
