## Standing rules the plan inherits

1. **Honesty gating is the brand** — numbers render only from live reads; quarterly honesty-debt sweeps.
2. **The protocol may not spend capital it has not earned** — spend-side features stay gated until a revenue line funds them.
3. **Minimal attack surface** — immutable contracts, verbatim forks, DELETE before ADD, no proxies.
4. **Order matters more than speed** — respect every "doing it early is worse than never" trap in `EVERYTHING_LEFT_2026_08_15.md`.
5. **Not on the menu** — perps, a stablecoin, RWA: one-stop-shop means completing swap + launch + lend + stake + govern on the existing flywheel.
