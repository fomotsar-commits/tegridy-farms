# Tegridy Farms — Deep Per-Contract Audit (2026-05-01)

**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Scope:** 22 production contracts (~21k LOC) on `main` post-MICROSCOPE_2026_04_30 + ~30 in-flight remediation commits
**Method:** 10 specialized auditor agents working in parallel, each instructed to **skip findings in MICROSCOPE_2026_04_30 and the per-contract sweep docs (001-101)** and hunt only for NEW vulnerabilities. Per-cluster reports under `.audit_101/DEEP_2026_05_01/`.

---

## 0. Executive verdict

**The microscope's prediction is confirmed.** Two days after MICROSCOPE_2026_04_30 catalogued 89 findings (5C/22H/39M/19L/4I) and the team shipped ~30 remediation commits, this deeper pass surfaces **143 NEW findings** — clustered tightly around the same architectural pattern the microscope already flagged: **half-installed mitigations** where one entrypoint receives a fix and a sibling does not.

**Total: 1 Critical · 24 High · 61 Medium · 47 Low · 10 Info — across the cluster set.**

**Three findings stand out as protocol-wide:**

1. **The microscope's C3 fix is structurally insufficient** (DEEP-GOV-01). The "current-power floor" (`votingPowerOf(msg.sender) > 0`) is a *binary* floor; a whale who divests 99.999% of their NFTs to a confederate post-snapshot still passes with a 1-wei sentinel and applies their **full pre-divest aggregate** at the vote site. The multi-NFT divestiture attack is still alive across all 4 governance contracts.
2. **`WETHFallbackLib` silently burns ETH when `to == address(0)`** (DEEP-LIB-H1). 12 production contracts route ETH through this lib. No upstream check is structural — a single forgotten initializer arg permanently destroys the contract balance with no revert and no event. Every importer is exposed; one-line lib fix closes it.
3. **The `kick()` permissionless decay primitive recommended by the microscope introduced 3 new bugs** (DS-01/02/03): `withdraw()` pre-decays before reward settlement, `kick()` zeroes boost without capturing pre-expiry rewards, and `kick()` runs during pause. Combined, any expired position is now a reward-burn target. The fix-of-the-fix is one commit but mandatory.

## 2. The five architectural patterns (and their unfixed instances)

### Pattern A — Half-installed mitigation / sibling miss
The microscope's §4 process recommendation ("every audit remediation should add a 'sibling search' check") was acknowledged but not adopted as practice. **15 of 24 Highs in this pass are sibling-miss patterns.**

| Microscope fix | Hardened entrypoint | Unfixed sibling |
|---|---|---|
| H10 (effectiveDeadline) | `TegridyLending` | `TegridyNFTLending.effectiveDeadline` (LD-H2) |
| H11 (pause-asymmetry interest) | `TegridyLending` | `TegridyNFTLending.repayLoan` (LD-H1) |
| `nonReentrant` on createOffer | `TegridyNFTLending.createOffer` | `TegridyLending.createLoanOffer` (LD-H3) |
| H17 / M-12 / M-30 (whenNotPaused) | one or two entries each | 9 owner mutators (DR-M-02), 3 router fns (DEEP-R-H02), `claimFees` (D-AMM-M3), `executeClaimRecovery` (DR-H-01), `kick` (DS-03), `notifyRewardAmount` (DS-05), Launchpad execute paths (DEEP-LP-02) |
| M-AMM1 (disabledPairs) | `harvest()` | `sync()` and `skim()` (D-AMM-H2) |
| M-D3 (acceptOwnership clears pending) | `TegridyDropV2` | `TegridyLaunchpadV2` (DEEP-LP-01) |
| H3 (TWAP first-obs admin-only) | first observation only | dormancy-bypass branch (D-AMM-H1) |
| `recoverStuckPrincipal` reservation | one entrypoint | `claimPendingUnsettled` (DR-01) |
| `_accrueBonusChecked()` tripwire | most accrual sites | `decayExpiredRestaker`, `emergencyForceReturn` inline (DR-03/06) |
| 009 M-1 salt-mining | acknowledged | NFTPoolFactory salt still missing chainid (DEEP-NFTPOOL-09) |
| M-Lib3 (`answer != 0`) | central `lib/SequencerCheck.sol` | `MemeBountyBoard._sequencerBuffer` duplicate (DEEP-LIB-H2) |
| H19 (setMintPrice 0) | zero branch | `setMintPrice(N>0)` and `configureDutchAuction` (DEEP-DROP-01/02) |
| 018 H-1 (mid-epoch gauge removal) | claimed-fixed | actually unfixed (DEEP-GOV-14) |

**Single-PR fix:** A standing pre-merge checklist line — *"Did you grep for siblings of this entrypoint? List them in the PR description."* Adopt as a CODEOWNERS-blocking review item.

### Pattern B — Pause is "user trade halt", not "protocol freeze"
Pause is supposed to be the universal kill-switch, but many entrypoints bypass `whenNotPaused`:
- **Revenue/POL** (DR-M-02): 9 owner mutators bypass pause (`executeHarvestLP`, `executeSweepETH`, `executeForfeitReclaim`, `reconcileRoundingDust`, `sweepDust`, `executeTokenSweep`, `autoReconcileDust`, `executeEmergencyWithdrawExcess`, `executeSweepTokens`)
- **Routers** (DEEP-R-H02): `distributeFeesToStakers`, `withdrawPendingDistribution`, `recoverCallerCredit`
- **AMM** (D-AMM-M3): `claimFees`, `executeSyncAccruedFees`, `proposeFeeChange`, `executeFeeChange`, `proposeDistributorChange`, `executeDistributorChange`
- **Staking** (DS-03/05): `kick`, `notifyRewardAmount`
- **Launchpad** (DEEP-LP-02): `executeProtocolFee`, `executeProtocolFeeRecipient`, `propose*` siblings
- **Lending** (LD-H1): NFTLending interest accrual

**Recommendation:** Audit invariant — every state-mutating external function carries `whenNotPaused` OR an explicit NatSpec line documenting why pause-bypass is intentional. Test every contract with a single forge invariant: `assert(paused() ⇒ no externally-callable mutator advances state).`

### Pattern C — Stale snapshot / lazy-decay window
The microscope's stale-checkpoint root cause (C3/C4) was meant to be closed by the `kick()` recommendation, but the implementation introduced fresh staleness:
- **DR-04**: `boostedAmountAt` stays cached after `staking.kick` zeros the staking-side boost — RevenueDistributor over-credits expired restakers in the lazy-decay window
- **DR-08**: `pendingBonus` view returns inflated boost for expired-not-yet-decayed positions
- **DR-10**: prior-checkpoint readback during lazy-decay window
- **DS-01**: `withdraw()` decays before settle, so the M-01 fix never fires on the most common exit path
- **DS-02**: `kick()` itself decays without settling — the pattern of record (Curve `LiquidityGaugeV4.kick`) settles user rewards FIRST

**Single-PR fix (DR-04 closes 3 findings):** Add `min(checkpointed, staking.current)` clamp inside `boostedAmountAt`. Closes DR-04, DR-08, DR-10 in one line.

### Pattern D — Microscope's C3 fix is structurally insufficient (DEEP-GOV-01)
The microscope recommended `require(votingPowerOf(msg.sender) > 0, "DIVESTED")` as a one-line patch closing the multi-NFT divestiture attack. **This was added at every vote site.** It's a *binary* floor — only rejects voters whose CURRENT power is exactly zero. A whale staking 50 small positions, snapshotting, transferring 49 to a confederate (paying 49 × ERC721 gas), retaining 1 sentinel, still passes the floor and applies their FULL pre-divest aggregate via `votingPowerAtTimestamp`. The microscope acknowledged this was the "one-line patch; long-term migrate to per-tokenId checkpoints" — long-term has not landed.

**Fix:** Replace `votingPowerOf > 0` with `min(historical, current) > 0` and apply the min-clamp to the power value used in vote tally:
```solidity
uint256 historical = votingEscrow.votingPowerAtTimestamp(msg.sender, snapshotTime);
uint256 current = votingEscrow.votingPowerOf(msg.sender);
uint256 power = historical < current ? historical : current;
if (power == 0) revert DivestedPosition();
```
Apply at all 4 governance contracts. Closes DEEP-GOV-01 across the cluster.

### Pattern E — Instant setters, no timelock
Same shape across drops, NFT pools, and AMM:
- **DEEP-DROP-01**: `configureDutchAuction` mid-DUTCH (no timelock, no phase gate)
- **DEEP-DROP-02**: `setMintPrice(N>0)` mid-PUBLIC (the H19 fix only closed the zero-toggle)
- **DEEP-NFTPOOL-03**: `proposeOwnerChange` has no timelock — defeats every other 24h timelock if owner key is compromised
- **DEEP-DROP-06**: `setBaseURI` mutability after partial mint (dynamic-trait sniping)

**Recommendation:** A single Pattern-E review pass: *"List every owner-only setter in this contract that is NOT behind TimelockAdmin. Justify each one in the audit doc, OR migrate it behind the timelock."* Currently `setRewardNotifier`, `setMintPrice`, `setMaxPerWallet`, `setBaseURI`, `configureDutchAuction`, `proposeOwnerChange` (NFTPool) all fail this gate.

## 4. Recommended deploy gating

**Block mainnet TVL increase until fixed:**

- **DS-01 (Critical)** — every expired-lock `withdraw()` permanently forfeits pending rewards. Defeats the AUDIT M-01 fix. Must ship.
- **All 24 Highs.** None are theoretical; every one has a concrete attack sequence in the per-cluster reports.

**Single-PR closures (highest leverage, ranked):**

1. **`if (to == address(0)) revert ZeroRecipient();` in WETHFallbackLib** — one line, closes silent-burn primitive across 12 importers (DEEP-LIB-H1).
2. **Reorder `TegridyStaking.withdraw()` and `kick()`** — settle rewards before decay. Closes Critical DS-01 + Highs DS-02/03 + Low DS-11. Mirrors Curve `LiquidityGaugeV4.kick`.
3. **Replace binary current-power floor with `min(historical, current)` clamp** at all 4 gov contracts — closes the still-alive multi-NFT divestiture attack (DEEP-GOV-01).
4. **`min(checkpointed, staking.current)` in `boostedAmountAt`** — closes 3 stale-cache findings in restaking (DR-04, DR-08, DR-10).
5. **Port H10 / H11 / nonReentrant fixes to TegridyNFTLending** — closes 3 Highs in lending (LD-H1, LD-H2, LD-H3) by making the two contracts converge architecturally.
6. **Add `whenNotPaused` to 9 owner mutators in RevenueDistributor + POLAccumulator** — closes DR-M-02 and addresses DR-H-01.
7. **Switch MemeBountyBoard's duplicate sequencer check to canonical `lib/SequencerCheck`** — closes DEEP-LIB-H2 by removing a duplicate impl entirely.
8. **Add `SequencerCheck.checkSequencerUp` to `claimDefaultedCollateral` / `claimDefault`** — closes DEEP-LIB-H3 (forced-liquidation-on-sequencer-resume).
9. **Gate TWAP dormancy-bypass branch behind `onlyOwner`** + add `adminResetPair` — closes 3 oracle Highs (D-AMM-H1, H3) and addresses H2/M5 partially.
10. **Phase-gate `setMintPrice(N)` and `configureDutchAuction` to `MintPhase.CLOSED`** — closes the entire instant-setter family in DropV2 (DEEP-DROP-01/02).

## 5. Process recommendations

The microscope said the team had "plateaued on AI sweeps + Spartan." This deep pass surfaces **143 NEW findings 2 days later** — implying the plateau was illusory: the audit pipeline finds Mediums/Lows fine but consistently misses the cross-contract semantic drift at the architectural layer.

Three concrete pipeline upgrades:

1. **Adopt the sibling-search lint as a CODEOWNERS-blocking review check.** Every audit-tagged commit must list, in the PR description, the sibling functions/contracts that share the same modifier surface. Reviewer cannot merge until the sibling list is either applied OR the PR explains why each sibling is actually different. This single discipline closes Pattern A — the largest source of recurring findings.

2. **Add forge invariant tests for the 5 cross-cluster invariants:**
   - `assert(paused() ⇒ no externally-callable mutator advances state)` — closes Pattern B
   - `assert(votingPowerOf(user) >= votingPowerAtTimestamp(user, ts) for all ts ≥ now - max_lock)` — fails today; would have caught DEEP-GOV-01 immediately
   - `assert(boostedAmountAt(user, ts) ≤ staking.boostedAmountAt(user, ts) for all ts)` — would catch DR-04
   - `assert(totalActivePrincipal == sum of restakers[user].positionAmount over active users)` — would catch DR-02
   - `assert(rewardDebt advances monotonically per-position)` — would catch DS-01/02

3. **Time-box the AI-sweep + invariant-fuzz combo at 6 weeks before deploy, then engage a paid firm.** OpenZeppelin / Trail of Bits / Spearbit / Cyfrin are the obvious candidates. The remaining surface (post-fix-of-fixes) is exactly the kind of cross-contract semantic drift a single-week-engagement firm catches in their first pass — but they cost nothing if invariant fuzzing has already cleared the bottom 40% of findings.
